this-organiser Privacy Policy

this-organiser is a task and work-organisation app made by SolwAI, based in Dumfries & Galloway, Scotland. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use this-organiser, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

For the purposes of UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018), SolwAI is the controller of the personal information described here, except where section 10 says otherwise.

  • Business name: SolwAI sole trader trading as SolwAI
  • Address: Queen of the South Arena, Dumfries, DG2 9BA
  • Email: privacy@solwai.co.uk

This policy covers the this-organiser web app, any mobile or desktop versions, and the this-organiser pages of the SolwAI website. Other SolwAI products have their own policies.

4. AI features

Some this-organiser features use AI, for example [suggesting tasks, summarising notes, breaking a project into steps, or prioritising your list]. When you use one of these features, the text needed for that request is sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to generate a result.

  • Only the content needed for that request is sent, and only when you use an AI feature.
  • Our AI provider processes it on our behalf under a data processing agreement and does not use it to train its models.
  • [The provider may keep request data for up to [30] days for abuse monitoring, then deletes it.]
  • AI suggestions can be wrong. Nothing an AI feature suggests is acted on until you accept it.
  • We do not use AI to make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

You can turn AI features off in Settings, and no content will then be sent to the AI provider.

5. Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers who help us run this-organiser, under written contracts that require them to protect it and use it only on our instructions.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or to a buyer if SolwAI's business is sold (you would be told first).

International transfers. Where a provider handles data outside the UK, we rely on UK adequacy regulations (for example, for the EU) or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum to protect it. Contact us for a copy of the safeguards.

7. Keeping it secure

We protect your information with measures suited to the risk, including:

  • encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and [at rest] for the database and backups
  • passwords stored using a strong one-way hash
  • access limited to the people who need it to run and support the service
  • regular backups, software updates and monitoring

No system is completely secure. If a breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will report it to the ICO within 72 hours of becoming aware of it and tell you without undue delay.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • access a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete
  • erase your information in certain circumstances
  • restrict how we use it in certain circumstances
  • object to uses based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time
  • data portability — receive your content in a reusable format
  • withdraw consent where we rely on it, without affecting earlier use

To use any of these rights, email [privacy@solwai.co.uk]. We will reply within one month, and there is normally no charge. We may need to confirm your identity first.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, please tell us first so we can try to put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or 0303 123 1113.

9. Cookies and local storage

this-organiser uses only the cookies and browser storage it needs to work: keeping you signed in, remembering your settings, and protecting against forged requests. These are strictly necessary, so they don't need your consent.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. If we use privacy-friendly analytics such as [tool], it does not use cookies or identify you.

If we ever add non-essential cookies, we will ask for your consent first.

10. Other people's information, children and changes

Other people's information. If you add details about other people, for example names in tasks [or people you share lists with], please only add what you need and make sure they would be comfortable with it. this-organiser is for personal use, so we don't offer business data processing agreements.

Children. this-organiser is not meant for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes. We may update this policy as the app or the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant, we will tell you by email or in the app before it takes effect.
