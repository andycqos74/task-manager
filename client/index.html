What is This-Organiser?

This-Organiser is a web-based task and project manager. It helps you capture tasks quickly, organise them into projects and goals, and plan your day and week.

  • Quick capture of tasks and notes
  • Projects and goals with deadlines, priorities and dependencies
  • A “My Day” list and rolling day and week planning
  • A Gantt timeline of your projects
  • Optional AI-assisted planning and prioritisation

You can create an account with an email address and password, or sign in with Google. Google sign-in is used only to confirm who you are: the app receives your name and email address to create and sign in to your account, and does not access any other data in your Google account.
